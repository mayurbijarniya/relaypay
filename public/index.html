Approval receipts sealed into each PayPal order

When the café approves, RelayPay seals what, how much, who and when into a receipt. Its SHA-256 fingerprint rides on the PayPal order as custom_id.

  • Orders over $100 need the café's extra tick
  • One edit breaks the fingerprint
